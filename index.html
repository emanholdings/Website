The Engineering Difference

The system comes second. Your energy usage comes first.

Most solar quotes start with a product — a panel, an inverter, a battery, a package size — and then try to make your property fit it.

We work the other way around. For a home, that starts with your power bill and how your household uses electricity through the day. For a business, it means looking at your consumption data, your operating hours, your site, your electrical infrastructure, and where things are headed. Only then do we work out whether solar makes sense, whether a battery is justified, and what size actually fits.

Bigger isn't always better. The right size is better.

Why It Matters

Too big wastes money. Too small leaves savings on the table.

A system that's oversized costs you more upfront than you needed to spend. A system that's undersized means you keep paying for grid power you could have avoided, and may need upgrading sooner than expected. Getting the size right means looking at today's load and any changes you can reasonably expect — an EV, a growing business — without trying to guess a decade ahead.
