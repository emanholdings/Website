Our Approach

We size the system to your bill, not the other way around

Most homeowners spend somewhere between $300 and $1,000 a month on electricity, and every household uses power differently — different appliances, different routines, different times of day. We don't sell fixed packages. We look at your bill and your usage, then size a system around what your home actually needs.

These are examples, not packages. Your system is sized around your bill and your usage.

As a rough guide, residential solar systems we work on typically range from around 6.6 kW up to 20 kW, with batteries up to around 50 kWh depending on the property. Where your system actually lands depends entirely on your usage.
