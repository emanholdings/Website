FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical residential install take?+

Most residential installations are completed in a single day, from mounting through to switch-on, subject to system size and roof access.

Do you handle government rebates and STCs?+

Yes — we manage the STC (Small-scale Technology Certificate) paperwork and apply the rebate directly to your quote, so you never have to chase it yourself.

What warranty comes with an installation?+

All installs include a 10-year workmanship warranty, on top of manufacturer warranties on panels, inverters and batteries (typically 10–25 years depending on the component).

Can I monitor my system from my phone?+

Yes — every system connects to our Smart Solar OS app, giving you live generation, usage and system health data from anywhere.
