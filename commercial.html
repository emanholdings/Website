Where We Focus

Commercial is the direction we're building this business around

If you run a factory, warehouse, pub, club or similar commercial property, electricity is likely one of your largest controllable costs. It's also one of the easiest things to get wrong when a system is designed around a product rather than your actual operation. Residential work remains an important part of what we do — but commercial is where our engineering background adds the most value, and it's the direction we're growing.

The Process

How a commercial project actually works

No jargon, no black box — here's what happens between an enquiry and a system going in.

1

Energy Assessment

We start by understanding you and your electricity costs — what you're paying, and why.

2

Site Assessment

We review your roof or installation area, the physical site, your electrical infrastructure, switchboard and any other site constraints.

3

Understand the Load

How much power you use, when you use it, and how that splits between day and night.

4

Design the System

We work out whether the project should involve solar only, solar plus battery storage, EV charging — or none of the above.

5

Run the Numbers

We assess whether the investment genuinely makes financial sense for your business.

6

Supply & Installation

We source the selected equipment and arrange installation using vetted, accredited installation partners.

7

Aftercare

We remain your single point of contact for the system, not a name you have to chase down later.
