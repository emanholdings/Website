Where We're At

We're a new business, and we're not going to pretend otherwise

Eman Holdings is a newly established company. We don't have a long list of completed projects to point to yet, and we're not going to invent one. What we do have is 25+ years of Ian Eman's electrical engineering background behind every system we design — see Meet Ian for the full story.

How We Operate

One point of contact. From design to installation and aftercare.

You engage us, we work out what you actually need, and then we source the equipment and arrange the installation. The physical electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed, accredited and vetted installation partners — that's a deliberate choice, not a shortcut. It means the trade work is done by people whose day-to-day job is installation, while we stay focused on getting the design right.

What doesn't change is who you deal with. We retain the customer relationship from the first conversation through to installation and aftercare. If something comes up with your system down the track, you're not left working out whether to call the electrician, the retailer, or a manufacturer — you call us, and we coordinate it.
