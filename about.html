About Eman Holdings

Engineering-led energy solutions, built on transparency and long-term performance.

Our Story

Built by engineers who believe energy should just work

Eman Holdings was founded to bring engineering discipline to the solar and energy industry. Too many households and businesses were sold systems that underperformed or went unmonitored for years. We set out to change that — combining quality hardware, careful system design and our own Smart Solar Operating System so every installation keeps performing long after the crew leaves site.

Eman Holdings is currently trading as our operating name while we complete our transition to a dedicated energy brand — Chartered Energy (Engineered Power Solutions) — pending ASIC registration. The people, the workmanship and the warranty behind every job stay exactly the same.
