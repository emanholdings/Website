Our Approach

A battery isn't always the right answer — and we'll say so

A battery makes the most sense when a meaningful share of your electricity use happens outside daylight hours, when reliability during outages matters, or when a business can shift load to make better use of stored solar. In other cases, the economics simply don't stack up yet.

If a battery doesn't make sense for your property, we'll tell you that instead of selling you one.

Where a battery is worthwhile, we currently work primarily with Sungrow battery systems — chosen for their track record in Australian conditions and local support, not because we're trying to sell a battery before we've looked at whether you need one.

For Homes

Batteries up to around 50 kWh, sized around your household's evening and overnight usage — and how that might change with an EV or higher household consumption over time.

For Businesses

Storage sized around your load profile — useful where night-time consumption is significant, where outages are costly, or where shifting load makes financial sense.
